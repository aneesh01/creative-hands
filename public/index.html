How it works

Pick. Paint. Glaze.

Pick

Browse the shelves and choose your piece — mugs, plates, figurines, home decor and more.

Paint

Grab a smock and a seat. Paints, brushes, stencils and stamps are all here waiting for you.

Glaze

Hand it over. We add the shine — in the kiln for ceramics, or with a glossy coat for plaster.

Two kinds of pieces. Which one’s yours?

Fired in our kiln

Ceramic pieces

Traditional ceramics like mugs and plates. Pick your palette from 50 glazes, then we glaze and fire your piece and call you when it’s ready.

  • Take it homeIn 1–2 weeks
  • Food safeYes, once fired
  • Best forThings you’ll use every day
Paint & take

Plaster pieces

Painted with acrylics and finished with a shiny acrylic glaze — plus our special mix of fine glitter if you want it. No kiln, no waiting.

  • Take it homeSame day
  • Glaze dries in30–60 minutes
  • Best forKids, parties, instant gratification

Our shelves are small! Please pick up ceramics as soon as we call. Plaster pieces are held for 72 hours.
