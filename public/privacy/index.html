Privacy

This website doesn't ask you to create an account, and we never sell information about our visitors.

Visitor statistics

To understand how people find us and which parts of the site are useful, we use:

  • Google Analytics, which records pages viewed and actions such as tapping “Call” or “Get directions”, along with general information like device type and approximate location (city level). It uses cookies. Google's own privacy policy is at policies.google.com/privacy, and you can opt out with Google's browser add-on.
  • Cloudflare Web Analytics, which counts visits without cookies and without identifying you.

We don't use this information for advertising, and we don't combine it with anything else about you.

Links to other services

Gift cards and our email list are handled by Square, and directions by Google Maps. Those services have their own privacy policies.
